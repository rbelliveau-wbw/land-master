## Backend findings and publication

1. **Check_for_Dates_Builder_T** uses expressions such as
   `rate != null && from == null || to == null`. The ungrouped final condition
   can reject an unused rate slot. It also stops at period seven and lacks
   chronological validation. A replacement script is prepared in
   [Check_for_Dates_Builder_T.dg](../../creator/workflows/Check_for_Dates_Builder_T.dg)
   for the existing workflow. It validates only populated slots, all twelve
   periods, matching boundaries and dates no later than purchase. It adds no
   forms or fields. Publication is separate from the frontend release.
2. **Calculate_Interest_Totals** initializes addlTax once before its lot loop
   and updates it only when the lot's Additional_Tax is non-null. A blank lot
   after a taxed lot can inherit its predecessor's value. The user's business
   interpretation of blank additional tax is pending. Frontend totals remain
   unavailable for that ambiguous selection and creation is blocked.
3. **createBuilderTakedown** now references Contract1 in its local extracted
   function, but the August export/generated Builder_Takedown schema does not
   verify that field. The existing lot/contract claims are preserved. No
   unverified Contract1 field was added to this widget payload.
4. **Set_Template_Defaults_Bui / Set_Lots_and_Subdiv_Build** are inactive in
   the committed export. The widget implements its own scoped defaults rather
   than relying on an inactive form-load workflow.
